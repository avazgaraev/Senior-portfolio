Let’s talk about your car
How long does ceramic coating take?

Our concept service allows two to three days for preparation, application, and initial curing. A real appointment timeline would depend on paint condition and the chosen coating.

What’s the difference between PPF and ceramic?

Paint protection film is a physical layer designed to help absorb minor road impacts. Ceramic coating focuses on surface gloss and easier cleaning. They can also be combined as part of a tailored finish.

Can I tailor a package to my vehicle?

Absolutely. The packages are starting points. A consultation would help shape the right combination around your car, its condition, and your priorities.

Can I request a consultation first?

Yes. Start with “Help me decide” in the care brief. We’ll preselect a consultation so you can discuss the right treatment after an inspection.
