# Gara Cars — studio hero

Asset: `hero-studio.png` (1672 × 941).

Created for this showcase using the built-in imagegen tool. This is generated concept imagery, not a photograph of a completed client project. The original output is preserved in the Codex generated-images folder; the website uses this local copy.

## Generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: photographic background for a premium automotive care website called Gara Cars. Generate a supremely photorealistic cinematic automotive campaign photograph, wide landscape 16:9. A beautiful unbranded graphite-black grand touring sports coupe, low and sculptural with round modern headlights, photographed from a very low front three-quarter angle, nose pointing left, car stretching across the lower half of the image, whole car visible with breathing room. Luxury black cyclorama studio with subtly reflective polished dark floor. A long narrow horizontal warm amber light slit in the far background at car-roof height; a single very soft overhead silver strip light carves out the hood, wheel arches, roofline and deep glossy paint. Highly realistic dark machined wheels and low profile tires. Dark charcoal and silver with ONLY a restrained amber accent. Upper 40 percent is near-black negative space for a large website headline, especially upper left. Visually expensive, minimalist, tactile, controlled reflections, subtle atmospheric depth, extremely sharp body details, no harsh blown highlights. No typography, no logos, no badges, no watermark, no humans, no clutter, no garage equipment, no neon cyberpunk, no smoke clouds. The vehicle must remain clearly readable against the dark studio.
