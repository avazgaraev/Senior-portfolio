    13 / A LITTLE CLARITY

    Good questions.
    Honest answers.

    Every project is different.
    Here is a helpful place to start.

    How long does an interior project take?+

    A single room may take 6–10 weeks from concept to installation. Full interiors typically need 3–6 months, depending on scope and bespoke production. Your schedule is agreed after the initial consultation.

    Do you produce custom furniture?+

    Yes. Kitchens, wardrobes, storage and individual pieces can be designed and produced for your space. Materials, dimensions and finish samples are agreed before production begins.

    Can you work from an existing design?+

    Absolutely. We can review your drawings and help with material selection, technical detailing, furniture production and installation. We first check what is already resolved and what still needs development.

    Do you handle measurement and installation?+

    Yes. Site measurement, technical checks and final installation are connected parts of the process. We confirm access, tolerances and site readiness before anything is made.

    Can I request an initial estimate online?+

    The project brief above helps establish scope, size, budget and style. On a live studio website, that would inform an initial estimate. In this showcase, you can download your brief; nothing is submitted.

    Do you work on commercial spaces?+

    Yes. Offices, hospitality and retail spaces can be developed around your brand, team and daily operations, with practical requirements considered from the first conversation.
