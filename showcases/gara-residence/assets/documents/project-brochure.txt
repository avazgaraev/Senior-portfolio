GARA RESIDENCE
Live above the ordinary.

A fictional residential concept in Baku, Azerbaijan.
Concept website by Gara Tech Software Solutions.

THE PROJECT
Two residential towers. Eighteen levels each. One private courtyard.
120 residences across floors 4–18, four apartments per floor per tower.
Levels 1–3 contain entrance, retail, shared amenities and garden spaces.
1–4 bedroom residences. 2.95 m ceiling height. Expected completion Q4 2027.

EVERYDAY LIFE
Private courtyard, fitness studio, resident lounge, underground parking,
children's garden, concierge, neighbourhood retail and controlled access.
