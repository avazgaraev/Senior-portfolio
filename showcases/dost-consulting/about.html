About DOST / Independent perspective

We do not start
with the service.
We start with the business.

DOST is a boutique strategic consulting concept based in Dubai, built around a simple belief: better decisions come from seeing the whole.

Clarity for businesses in motion.

A useful kind of independence

Sometimes the most valuable
thing a consultant can say
is “not yet.”

A business may ask for social media when its position is unclear. A founder may want a new market when the current model needs better economics. A company may ask about Dubai when another location would better serve its customers.

Good consulting creates room to examine the question. We connect the commercial, brand and operating perspectives before recommending what deserves your attention, your time and your capital.
