Social media / A business tool

A channel needs
a commercial purpose.

We begin with the business goal. Content follows the customer, the position and a credible path to conversion.

Business goal→Customer→Positioning→Message→Channel→Content→Conversion

Social media can build awareness, answer objections or start a sales conversation. Its role depends on how your customer actually makes a decision.

The useful question is not how many posts to publish. It is what the channel should change for the business, and how we will recognise progress.
