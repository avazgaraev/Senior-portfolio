With credit.

GARA FRAME is a fictional photography studio and a design showcase by Gara Tech Software Solutions. Event names, dates, locations and studio statistics are illustrative. The people pictured are models or subjects of the original photographers, and are not identified as actual GARA FRAME clients.

Photographs are sourced from Pexels and used under the Pexels License. These photographs were not taken by GARA FRAME or Gara Tech. The collections are art-directed fictional stories, not records of actual events in Baku.
