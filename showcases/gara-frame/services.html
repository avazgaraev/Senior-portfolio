Before we begin

A few good questions.

How far ahead should we enquire?

As soon as you have a date or a season in mind. For conferences and large events, an early conversation gives us time to plan the right coverage together.

We are not used to being photographed. Is that okay?

More than okay. We give clear, gentle direction when it helps, and leave plenty of room for you to be yourself. You don’t need to arrive knowing what to do.

Do you photograph outside Baku?

Yes. We shape travel and coverage around the location of your occasion. Tell us where you have in mind when you make your enquiry.

How will we receive our photographs?

Through a personal online gallery, with a carefully edited story of your day. Albums and additional print selections can be discussed as part of your collection.
